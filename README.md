# *An Introduction to 3D Printing*
A free and beginner-friendly educational slide presentation covering the fundamentals of 3D printing.  It balances theory and practical advice, providing a general introduction that applies across a wide range of modern FFF (FDM) printers.  It is broken into 3 sections:
- Part 1: process, limitations, and advantages.
- Part 2: examples, critical thinking, and design.
- Part 3: investigating physics with a 3D printer.

This is not a setup or tuning guide for your specific printer model.  For help setting up a new printer, consult your manufacturer's documentation.  

Several screenshots and examples were created with PrusaSlicer simply because it is the software I know best.  However, the underlying concepts and workflow still apply to other slicers.

In addition to this guide, I recommend participating in the 3D printing community.  It is a great place to ask questions, share ideas, find inspiration, and explore new ideas.
